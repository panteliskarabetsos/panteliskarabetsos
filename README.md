### Featured

**[Adversarial Attacks to Evade ML-based NIDS](https://github.com/panteliskarabetsos/Adversarial-attacks)** · diploma thesis
Evasion attacks (FGSM, PGD, ZOO, HopSkipJump) against an MLP and a Random Forest intrusion detector
trained on CICIDS2017, run both unrestricted and limited to 30 timing and packet-size features.
The constraint lowered attack success in every setting but removed none of it: 45.5–78.6% of attacked
malicious flows were still classified as benign. Also measures cross-model transferability.
`Python` `PyTorch` `scikit-learn` `Adversarial Robustness Toolbox`
